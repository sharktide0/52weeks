# Handoff: Lane's 52 Skills in 52 Weeks Challenge

## Who I am
Lane Bruns, 19, finance freshman at Purdue (Daniels School of Business, Honors College), from Carmel, IN. Catholic my whole life. No engineering background. Long-term goal is my own personal financial planning firm. Studying for the FINRA SIE over Christmas break 2026.

## How to talk to me
- Casual and short, match my texting tone.
- Plain writing. No em dashes, no flattery, no hedging, no three-item lists.
- Name exact resources, books, tools and costs. Nothing vague.
- Search instead of guessing. Don't offer to look things up, just do it.
- Skip obvious warnings and basic disclaimers.

## The challenge
Learn one new skill every week for 52 weeks. Goal is becoming well rounded, not "expert." Every week has a hard "done when" finish line so it can't turn into just watching YouTube.

- Runs Mon Nov 23, 2026 through the week of Nov 15, 2027.
- 52 main skills, one per week.
- 6 background (BG) habits stacked on top of any week: journaling, meditation, cold showers, phone detox, wake at 5am, flexibility.
- Week 1 is the personal website. It hosts an About page, resume, and a 52 Week Challenge page with a log updated every Sunday. It goes on LinkedIn Featured with an announcement post.
- Possibly filming it as a content series.

## Scheduling logic
- Light skills during finals (Dec 7, May 3) and Christmas break (SIE studying).
- Coin magic on Christmas week, performed for family, then combined with week 2's card tricks into one routine.
- Electronics fundamentals (wk 14) comes right before PCB design (wk 15) because it builds the 555 circuit the PCB uses.
- Write a book over spring break (wk 17, Mar 15).
- Taxes the week of Apr 5, before Apr 15.
- Outdoor and hands-on skills over summer.
- People skills in fall when back on campus (CoRec climbing wall, improv via BoilerLink, public speaking, debate).
- Guitar song is the finale (wk 52). I already play acoustic guitar.
