# Site brief: lanebruns (working name)

What goes on each page and what it has to do. No layout, colors or fonts here. Those are yours.

**Audience:** finance professionals and financial planners, mostly arriving from LinkedIn, often on a phone.
**Job of the site:** show who Lane is, and make the 52 week challenge easy to follow week to week.
**Built with:** plain HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages with a custom domain. Each page has its own link, so LinkedIn can point straight at 52 Weeks.

Items marked *(optional)* are suggestions you can cut.

---

## On every page

**Header**
- Your name (links home)
- Nav, in this order: Lane, Projects, Accomplishments, 52 Weeks
- Shows which page you're on
- Phone version of the nav (fits on one line, or a menu button)

**Footer**
- Name
- LinkedIn, email
- *(optional)* "Updated every Sunday" or last updated date
- *(optional)* Small "this week: [skill]" line so every page points to the challenge

---

## 1. Lane (home)

Purpose: who you are in 10 seconds, then send people to the challenge.

**Content**
- Headshot (you supply)
- Name
- One-line title (e.g. "Finance student at Purdue")
- Short bio, 2 to 4 sentences, in your own words
- Quick facts:
  - School: Purdue, Daniels School of Business, Honors College
  - Hometown: Carmel, IN
  - Studying for: FINRA SIE
  - Goal: own personal financial planning firm
- Links: LinkedIn, email, resume (PDF download)
- "This week" box: week number, skill, done-when line, link to 52 Weeks. It updates by itself.

**Optional features**
- Progress indicator for the year (e.g. "Week 8 of 52" or a 52-box strip)
- Latest video thumbnail from the challenge
- "Why I'm doing this" short paragraph
- Faith or personal-values line, if you want that on there
- Photo gallery or a second, more casual photo

## 2. Projects

Purpose: things you've actually built or made.

**Content, per project**
- Image (screenshot or photo, you supply)
- Title
- 1 to 2 sentence description
- What you used (tools)
- Status: done / running / planned
- Link (live thing, GitHub, PDF or video), if there is one

**Starting list**
- CoRec tracker (Python + GitHub Actions)
- This website (week 1)
- Daily data script that texts or emails you (week 8)
- 555 timer circuit + KiCad PCB (weeks 14 to 15)
- DCF valuation model (week 38)
- Figma app design (week 36)
- *(optional)* Options paper-trading log (week 11), mock 1040 (week 20), Social Security claiming analysis (week 21). These are strong for a finance audience.

**Optional features**
- Filter by type (Finance / Tech / Build)
- Click a project to open a detail view with more photos
- Planned projects shown faded or in a separate "coming up" group

Purpose: a dated record of wins.

**Content, per entry**
- Date (month + year)
- Title
- One-line description
- *(optional)* proof link or image (certificate, photo, score)

**Starting list (add yours)**
- Purdue Honors College, Daniels School of Business (Fall 2026)

**Optional features**

- Category labels (Academic / Certifications / Challenge / Personal)
- Pull challenge wins in automatically when a week is marked done

## 4. 52 Weeks

Purpose: the main event. Someone should know what you're doing this week within 3 seconds.

**Section A: Intro**
- Title (e.g. "52 Skills in 52 Weeks")
- What the challenge is, 2 to 3 sentences (one skill a week, hard finish line, video each week)
- Dates: Nov 23, 2026 to Nov 21, 2027
- Year progress (week X of 52, or a 52-box strip)

**Section B: This week (biggest thing on the page)**
- "THIS WEEK" + week number
- Skill name
- Done-when line
- Day-by-day plan (Mon to Sun), with today highlighted
- Resources and cost
- Countdown to Sunday's video
- Space where the video goes once it's posted

**Section C: Past weeks**
- One item per finished week, newest first
- Each shows: video thumbnail, week number, skill, dates
- Clicking opens: the video, done-when, your journal entry, finished date
- Week 1 has no video. The site itself is the proof.

**Section D: Still ahead**
- Every remaining week: number, skill, start date
- *(optional)* category (Tech, Money, Mind, Body...)
- *(optional)* grouped by month

**Section E: Background habits**
- The six: journaling, meditation, cold showers, phone detox, wake at 5am, flexibility
- Done-when for each
- *(optional)* checkmark once you've done that habit's week

**States the design has to handle**
- Before Nov 23: "Starts Monday" with a countdown, nothing in Past weeks yet
- Mid-challenge: normal
- A week that's over but the video isn't up yet
- After week 52: "Finished" wrap-up

**Optional features**
- Filter past weeks by category
- Stats: skills done, hours, money spent
- Highlight reel or "favorite 5" once a few are done
- Subscribe or follow link (LinkedIn) so people get Sunday updates
